What's new

Version 1.0 · October 2026

  • Ranks and medals. Earn XP for every letter, image and website you wreck. Twelve ranks, Lurker to Web Wrecker, each with its own medal.
  • Daily challenges. Three new ones every day, easy to hard, about the sites you destroy and the guns you use.
  • New guns. A cluster launcher that splits into eight bomblets, and a pulsar whose jets sweep a disc out of the page.
  • Rail laser. Charge it, then one pink beam cuts a trench to the edge of the page.
  • Gravity well. It tears the page apart like a whirlpool and ends in a heavy burst.
  • Uzi. Replaces the pistol: full-auto and cuts through letters.
  • The page burns. The flamethrower sets the page alight and fire spreads across it. Every blast leaves a ring of fire.
  • Worlds behind the page. Synthwave city, dusk mountains, sunny hills or deep space, a different one for each site.
  • Reload bars. Each heavy weapon reloads on its own; the nuke takes 9 seconds.
  • Finishing a page. A page counts as destroyed at 70% of its letters and 70% of its content. The results float over the page and you can keep going.
  • Big blasts no longer stall the game, the jetpack never runs out, and rockets go off right where you aim.
